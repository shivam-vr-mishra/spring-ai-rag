ACME Robotics - Information Security Policy

SECTION 1: PASSWORD AND ACCESS REQUIREMENTS

All employee accounts must use a password manager issued by the IT department.
Passwords must be at least 16 characters. Password rotation is not required;
rotation on a fixed schedule was discontinued in 2024 because it encouraged
weak, incremental passwords.

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for every system that touches
customer data. Hardware security keys are required for anyone with production
database access. SMS-based codes are explicitly forbidden as a second factor.

SECTION 2: DATA CLASSIFICATION

ACME classifies all data into three tiers.

RESTRICTED data includes customer robot telemetry, payment details, and
employee records. It may never leave ACME-controlled infrastructure and must
be encrypted at rest using AES-256.

INTERNAL data includes engineering documentation, roadmaps, and meeting notes.
It may be stored in approved cloud services but must not be shared externally.

PUBLIC data includes marketing material and published documentation. No
restrictions apply.

SECTION 3: LAPTOP AND DEVICE RULES

Full-disk encryption is mandatory on every laptop. Lost or stolen devices must
be reported to security@acme.example within one hour of discovery, regardless
of the time of day or day of week.

Personal USB storage devices may not be connected to work laptops. This is
enforced technically through endpoint management, not merely by policy.

SECTION 4: VENDOR AND THIRD-PARTY ACCESS

Any third-party vendor requiring access to RESTRICTED data must complete a
security review before access is granted. The review takes approximately three
weeks and requires a signed data processing agreement.

Vendor access is time-boxed to 90 days and must be explicitly renewed. Access
that is not renewed expires automatically.
