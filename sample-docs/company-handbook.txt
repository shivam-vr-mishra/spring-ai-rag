ACME Robotics - Internal Engineering Handbook

SECTION 1: DEPLOYMENT POLICY

All production deployments at ACME Robotics must occur between 10:00 and 16:00
on Tuesday, Wednesday, or Thursday. Friday deployments are strictly forbidden
because the on-call rotation changes at 18:00 on Fridays and the incoming
engineer will not have context on the change.

Every deployment requires two approvals: one from the owning team's tech lead,
and one from the Site Reliability Engineering on-call. The SRE approval may be
waived for changes that touch only documentation or test files.

SECTION 2: INCIDENT SEVERITY LEVELS

SEV-1 means complete loss of service for all customers. The response time
target is 5 minutes and it pages the entire on-call tree including the VP of
Engineering.

SEV-2 means degraded service or complete loss for a single large customer.
The response time target is 15 minutes.

SEV-3 means a minor bug with a known workaround. It is handled during normal
business hours and does not page anyone.

SECTION 3: THE ROBOT FLEET

ACME operates three robot models in the field. The Hauler-7 is a warehouse
transport unit with a 400 kilogram payload capacity and a 9 hour battery life.
The Picker-3 is a shelf-picking arm with sub-millimeter precision, rated for
2 million cycles before servicing. The Scout-1 is an inspection drone with a
42 minute flight time and thermal imaging.

The Hauler-7 firmware must never be updated over the air while the unit is
carrying a payload. Doing so has caused three separate SEV-2 incidents.

SECTION 4: ON-CALL COMPENSATION

Engineers on the primary on-call rotation receive a stipend of 800 dollars per
week. Secondary on-call receives 300 dollars per week. Any engineer paged more
than four times in a single night is entitled to the following day off.
